The Developer Portal is used daily by other engineers. It connects to our databases, the Kubernetes cluster, and the GitLab Runner CI agents, and brings what developers need into one place.

## Faster access fixes

Developers can look up a user's session, see whether they have the right access, grant it, and reset the session, all from the same screen. Simple access issues that used to take a while became quick.

## Logs without kubectl

Pod logs for every application are a click away. That helps developers who aren't familiar with Kubernetes, and anyone who just wants the logs without logging into the cluster, finding the right pod, and sorting through output.

## Faster deploys

With the information gathered in one place, the team deployed projects noticeably faster.
